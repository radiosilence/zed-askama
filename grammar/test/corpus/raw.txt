================================================================================
Raw content keeps tags as text
================================================================================

{% raw %}{{ not an expression }}{% if %}{% endraw %}

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(template
  (content)
  (raw_block
    (raw_tag)
    (raw_content)
    (endraw_tag))
  (content))

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    (raw_content)
    (endraw_tag))
