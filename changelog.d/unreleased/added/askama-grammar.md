- **Askama templates are highlighted and structured in Zed.** Zed had no Askama language, so templates opened as plain HTML or text; now `.askama` and `.rinja` files (with `.md.askama` as Markdown and `.txt.askama` as text) get a tree-sitter grammar written against askama 0.16.1's parser, with highlighting, injected HTML or Markdown, matched block tags, indentation, an outline of blocks, macros and includes, vim text objects and snippets.
